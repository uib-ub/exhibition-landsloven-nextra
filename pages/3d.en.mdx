# 3D model of the exhibition

The Laws of the Land jubilee exhibition was first and foremost an installation of archaeological objects, written sources, explanatory texts, and illustrative images on the exhibition hall of the second floor of the museum at Bryggen in Bergen. After this exhibition closed, what remains is this webpage where a 3D model of the original display can be navigated by those who wish to relive the moment. The model was created by Anders Dragesund of Bymuseet i Bergen.
